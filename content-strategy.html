Phase 3

Content Strategy - Justin Welsh

Prepared: 2026-05-16 For: Justin Welsh (@thejustinwelsh) Scope: Content audit + additions that compound the cross-sell, VSL, and scorecard surfaces. Not a rewrite of Saturday Solopreneur. Not a takeover of LinkedIn. The work sits on top of what already runs.

The frame

Justin's content engine is built. Saturday Solopreneur ships every Saturday at 175K+ subscribers. LinkedIn long-form runs daily across a 1M+ audience. X / Twitter runs daily across a ~572K audience. Cross-platform repurposing is already systematised (Content OS is the productised version of this).

Three things compound the funnel layer without disrupting the editorial:

  1. A monthly Saturday Solopreneur "Operator Issue" that pre-sells the Creator MBA Masterclass via the new VSL bridge.
  2. A LinkedIn / X content lane that surfaces the scorecard as the primary CTA for 1-2 posts per week.
  3. A long-form authority lane (YouTube own-channel, or expanded podcast feature cadence) that walks the operator-stage frameworks the Creator MBA teaches.

This is not "post more." It is "the existing cadence routes into the funnel surfaces we install."

Lane 01 - Monthly Saturday Solopreneur Operator Issue

What it is: One Saturday issue per month explicitly routed to the Creator MBA VSL. Same length, same voice, same cadence. The CTA at the bottom of that issue is the VSL bridge URL, not the next Saturday issue. The other three issues per month run editorial as normal.

Topic frame: The monthly Operator Issue picks one specific bottleneck a stage-2-or-3 solopreneur hits ("audience-to-revenue conversion stalls past month 12," "course pricing under-shoots when audience is large but operator is solo," "the LinkedIn-to-newsletter handoff is broken when the LinkedIn audience is 10x the newsletter," etc) and walks a 4-5 minute read on why the bottleneck exists, then routes the reader to the Creator MBA VSL with a one-sentence transition. The transition is voice-locked to Saturday Solopreneur, not a marketing handoff.

Cadence: Monthly. Saturday-issue slot 1 or slot 3 of the month works best (avoid the last Saturday of the month so Creator MBA enrolment windows do not collide with end-of-month cash crunches in the audience).

What AG1 builds: - The Operator Issue topic bank (12 issues, one per month for a year) outlined and queued - The closing-sentence template that hands off to the VSL without breaking voice - The pixel + UTM setup that tracks Operator-Issue-driven Creator MBA conversions separately from organic LinkedIn / X traffic - The replay sequence for subscribers who clicked but did not enrol (3 touches over 5 days)

Year-1 floor: Roughly 12 Operator Issues hitting a 175K list, each driving Creator MBA traffic to the VSL bridge. Conservative single-issue conversion math compounds into a meaningful Creator MBA revenue line on its own.

Lane 02 - LinkedIn / X scorecard-surface content

What it is: 1-2 LinkedIn or X posts per week that surface the Solopreneur Stage Scorecard as the primary CTA. Topic frame is operator-tier ("are you a stage-1, stage-2, or stage-3 solopreneur and you do not know the answer? take the scorecard"), positioned next to Justin's existing operator-tone content.

Why this exists: The 1M+ LinkedIn audience currently routes to newsletter capture. The scorecard adds a second route - one with a per-respondent readiness signal that pre-qualifies the inbound. Some of that scorecard-routed traffic ends up at the newsletter anyway (low-score routes to newsletter). Some routes to the Operating-System courses (mid-score). Some routes to a 1-1 inquiry call (high-score, 80+).

Cadence: 1-2 posts per week, mixed into the existing LinkedIn / X cadence. Not replacing. Adding.

What AG1 builds: - The scorecard surface (12 questions, score reveal, routing logic, email capture) - The 24-hook content bank that drives scorecard traffic (hooks like "the question that tells you whether you are a stage-1 or stage-3 solopreneur," "the four-line test for whether your newsletter is monetised or just busy," "the thing every solopreneur googles before they admit they need help") - The Linktree / LinkedIn-bio / X-bio update so the scorecard is the second primary CTA alongside the newsletter - The application route on the back of the high-score reveal, so the 1-1 inquiry lane has a clean inbound flow

Year-1 floor: Even modest scorecard-completion rates on a 1M+ LinkedIn audience drive low-thousands of incremental newsletter subscribers in 90 days. The high-score branch fills a tightly capped 1-1 inquiry lane that prices in the high-ticket range and adds six figures on its own across Year 1.

Lane 03 - Long-form authority (own-channel or expanded podcast feature cadence)

What it is: Two options here. Pick one, run it for 12 weeks, measure.

Option A - Own-channel YouTube. Currently Justin appears on third-party channels (Peter Yang's "$8M Business with 0 Employees" feature is the strongest public surface). A weekly own-channel YouTube long-form (15-25 min, talking-head + screen share) at Saturday-issue cadence walks the operator-stage frameworks the Creator MBA teaches. The episodes are evergreen, search-indexed, and embedable on the Creator MBA landing page.

Option B - Expanded podcast feature cadence. Continue appearing on third-party channels at 2x current cadence, with a structured CTA on every feature that routes to the scorecard. Outsource the booking. Justin shows up and talks.

Why this exists: The audience exists across LinkedIn, X, and newsletter. The long-form authority layer is the missing surface. Long-form video earns the highest-ticket inquiries because viewers self-select into 25-minute commitments. A Creator MBA buyer is much more likely to enrol after a long-form than after a 90-second LinkedIn post.

Cadence: Weekly for 12 weeks, then re-evaluate.

What AG1 builds: - Topic bank (24 episodes outlined - 12 if option B) - Production playbook (talking-head + screen-share setup, recording day cadence, editor handoff) - Each episode wired with a CTA to either the VSL bridge or the scorecard - Description and timestamp templates so episodes get search traction on launch day

Year-1 floor: Option A in particular compounds for the life of the channel. Even modest channel growth at 12 weeks of weekly drops is in the low-thousands of subscribers. Each long-form episode is a perpetual asset that re-routes traffic into the funnel.

What does not change

  • The Saturday Solopreneur editorial (voice, topic selection, structure) - untouched
  • LinkedIn long-form daily cadence - untouched
  • X / Twitter daily cadence - untouched
  • Sponsor inventory on the newsletter - untouched
  • The Operating-System course pages on Kajabi (LinkedIn OS, Content OS) - untouched on the landing-page level, only the cross-sell layer changes
  • The Creator MBA Masterclass curriculum - untouched, AG1 builds the VSL bridge and the landing page but not the curriculum

The 24-hook scorecard-and-Creator-MBA bank (LinkedIn / X cross-platform)

Used to drive scorecard traffic from the LinkedIn 1M+ audience. Pulls from operator-tier framings already in Justin's voice. Mix of contrarian, naming-the-stage, and audit-style hooks.

  1. The question that tells you whether you are a stage-1, stage-2, or stage-3 solopreneur.
  2. Your newsletter has 50K subscribers and is not monetised. Here is the math on why.
  3. The four-line test for whether your LinkedIn-to-newsletter handoff is broken.
  4. Three things every $8M solo operator has and the rest do not.
  5. The thing every solopreneur googles before they admit they need help.
  6. The post-product moment where every solopreneur stalls and how to recognise it.
  7. Why most one-person businesses cap at $200K and the four moves that take them past it.
  8. The audience size at which a self-paced course outgrows its sales page.
  9. The cross-sell every productised solopreneur is leaving on the table.
  10. The bridge that has to exist between a 100K newsletter and a high-ticket course.
  11. The application-vs-self-serve question every productised creator has to answer.
  12. The reason "built without ads" has a ceiling and the reason that ceiling is fine for some operators.
  13. The two-line audit that tells you whether your funnel is wired or just stacked.
  14. The metric that matters when audience is bigger than revenue.
  15. The post-purchase sequence every Kajabi-and-Stripe creator is missing.
  16. The order bump that converts at 15-25% on a self-paced course.
  17. The four signs your audience is asking for a higher tier and you have not built it.
  18. The reason your highest-ticket buyers do not buy from your sales page.
  19. The 12-question scorecard that returns your solopreneur stage in 90 seconds.
  20. The audit I would run on a one-person business with a 100K-plus newsletter and three products.
  21. The five-minute test for whether your funnel infrastructure is the bottleneck.
  22. The reason your second course launches at 60% of your first course revenue and how to fix it.
  23. The handoff that is missing between every long-form post and every Kajabi checkout.
  24. The before-and-after of installing the cross-sell layer on a three-product creator portfolio.

Each hook routes to the scorecard or the VSL bridge, depending on intent. AG1 ships the routing logic so the CTA reflects the topic.

Photoshoot and b-roll brief

Single half-day photoshoot in Justin's working setup (one-person business, home office or co-working surface, Saturday morning lighting). Outputs:

  • 6-8 hero shots for the landing page (talking-head, working-at-desk, walking-with-coffee, in-call setup)
  • 12-15 supporting shots for newsletter graphics, LinkedIn carousels, Creator MBA pre-sell creatives
  • 90 seconds of b-roll for the VSL (intercut over the talking-head)

Brief: minimalist, light-led, on-brand for "one-person business." No agency-stock aesthetic. No corporate stiffness. The shoot is shot inside Justin's working day.
