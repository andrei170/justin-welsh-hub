Fastest Install
01

Three Kajabi Checkouts. No Bundle, No Order Bump, No Post-Purchase Cross-Sell.

LinkedIn OS and Content OS are two separate Stripe checkouts on Kajabi. A buyer of one is never offered the other at checkout. The Creator MBA Masterclass, when it goes live, becomes the third isolated checkout unless the cross-sell layer ships first. The fix is bundle pricing at Kajabi, an order bump on each individual product, and a 5-touch post-purchase email sequence from each product into the next tier up. The hardest part of a sale is acquiring the buyer; you have done that work tens of thousands of times across LinkedIn OS alone. The cross-sell layer adds revenue per existing buyer at near-zero acquisition cost. Conservatively a 25-35% AOV lift on the two live products and the equivalent on Creator MBA at launch.

Cost of Waiting

Mid-six-figures Year-1 in incremental cross-sell revenue on the existing product portfolio plus the Creator MBA launch flow. Each month the cross-sell does not exist is one month of compounding AOV not captured.

6 months from now - half of that floor never captured, plus the Creator MBA launches into a portfolio with no cross-sell already wired.

Fix - Bundle pricing in Kajabi + order bump on each product + 5-touch post-purchase sequence into the next tier. Day 1 to Day 3 of install.
Highest Revenue Lever
02

175K Saturday Solopreneur Subscribers And No VSL Bridge Into Creator MBA Masterclass.

The newsletter signup confirmation routes to the next Saturday issue. When the newsletter promotes Creator MBA in a Saturday issue, the click goes to a static Kajabi sales page, not to a VSL that warms the subscriber before checkout. A 6-9 minute VSL placed before the Creator MBA sales page converts a warm newsletter subscriber at a meaningfully higher rate. The 175K list is the warmest possible audience for the Creator MBA when it launches, and the conversion surface between them is currently absent. The CMBA2026FLASHSALE coupon code on the homepage tells you Creator MBA is closer to launch than the "Coming Soon" framing implies. The VSL bridge has to ship before launch, not after.

Cost of Waiting

High-six-figures Year-1 on the bridged-VSL path versus the static-page path, concentrated in the first 90 days post-launch. The lift compounds as Creator MBA launch list size grows.

6 months from now - the Creator MBA launches without the VSL surface, and the conversion-rate ceiling locks at the static-page rate for the life of the product.

Fix - 6-9 minute Creator MBA bridge VSL + dedicated landing page + ConvertKit / Kajabi-routed nurture from newsletter signup. Day 4 to Day 7 of install.
Strategic Ceiling
03

1M+ LinkedIn Audience And No Scorecard Or 1-1 Inquiry Route Above The Productised Tier.

LinkedIn routes warm viewers to newsletter capture as the primary CTA. The newsletter routes to the Operating-System courses and, when it launches, the Creator MBA Masterclass. There is no scorecard, no application form, no 1-1 inquiry route. The operator-tier reader who has completed both OS courses, read the newsletter for 18 months, and wants a higher-touch lane has nowhere to go. A 12-question "Solopreneur Stage Scorecard" plus an application-gated 1-1 inquiry lane on the back of an 80+ score opens the tier above the productised ceiling without you having to build a new course. Cap is your call; the application form pre-qualifies hard.

Cost of Waiting

Six-figures Year-1 on the 1-1 line alone at a tightly capped 5-10 inquiries per quarter at high-ticket pricing. The scorecard email-capture compounds for life of the list, conservatively low-thousands of incremental subscribers from the existing audience in the first 90 days.

6 months from now - half the 1-1 floor never captured, plus the email asset is 6 months smaller than it should be when the inquiry lane opens.

Fix - 12-question Solopreneur Stage Scorecard on ScoreApp / Typeform + application-gated 1-1 inquiry route on the high-score branch. Day 4 to Day 7 of install.
