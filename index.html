Doesn't a Cohort tier violate the one-person-business positioning?

No. The Cohort is a quarterly four-time-a-year delivery, two days in person, 90-day implementation built around your existing weekly cadence. It does not require an employee. It does not require an ongoing fulfilment team you have to manage. The build team handles ops, AV, venue, screening, and the 90-day implementation infrastructure. You show up, run the room, and step out. Operationally it sits closer to a "speaking engagement four times a year" than a recurring fulfilment commitment. The positioning is "I built this without hiring," not "I never sell anything outside a self-paced course." Sahil Bloom runs his mastermind and still publishes a free newsletter without contradiction. Same architecture works here.

How much of my time does this need?

One day to film the three VSLs (Creator MBA, LinkedIn OS, Content OS) in a single studio block. One day to film the Cohort positioning content. Calendar holds for cohort delivery in Lever 02 (two days in person plus bi-weekly group calls for 90 days, four times a year). The rest is build team work plus you reviewing copy, creative, and pricing decisions before they ship. Most operators at your stage spend under 8 hours a month during the 90-day build, and under 2 hours a month after the system is in production. Once we hit Lever 02, plan on the four cohort-event weeks per year as the only structural commitment beyond the existing newsletter.

Will the Solopreneur Operator Cohort cannibalise the Creator MBA?

No. The Cohort and Creator MBA solve different problems. Creator MBA is self-paced curriculum for solopreneurs building toward $250K. The Cohort is strategic operating-room access for solopreneurs who already cleared the curriculum, crossed $250K-$1M ARR, and want to scale to $1M+ without hiring. Different deliverable, different audience, different buying motion. Most Cohort applicants will be Creator MBA graduates moving up the ladder, not net-new buyers choosing Cohort over Creator MBA. Where there's overlap, the Cohort is the explicit "post-Creator-MBA" tier. It expands the ladder, it doesn't compete inside it.

How does this work with the Saturday Solopreneur sponsorship revenue?

Sponsorship-revenue track stays untouched. The main weekly send to all 175K+ subscribers stays as-is. The segmented post-purchase nurture in Lever 01 runs as a separate flow off Creator MBA, LinkedIn OS, and Content OS purchase events, not against the main newsletter list. Sponsors don't notice the difference, the open rate on the main send doesn't move, and the segmented buyer flows operate underneath in parallel. The build adds revenue lines, it doesn't touch the existing ones.

The Cohort is sized at 6-10 seats first, sold to existing Creator MBA alumni who've crossed $250K+ ARR (the warmest list for this offer that exists).
