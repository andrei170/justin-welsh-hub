Phase map (less than 7 days from kickoff)

Phase One - Days 1 to 3 - Cross-sell layer ships first

Why first: The cross-sell layer is the lowest-risk install. It touches existing live products only. It does not gate any new offer launch. Money in the bank inside the first week.

Day 1: - Kickoff working session. Confirm Kajabi access. Confirm ESP. Confirm Stripe access (read-only). Confirm Justin's preferences on bundle pricing range. - Bundle pricing tier configured in Kajabi (LinkedIn OS + Content OS combined at the bundle rate) - Order bump configured on each individual product checkout

Day 2: - 5-touch post-purchase email sequence written (LinkedIn OS to Content OS) - 5-touch post-purchase email sequence written (Content OS to LinkedIn OS) - ConvertKit / Kajabi tagging logic mapped

Day 3: - Sequences live in ESP / Kajabi - Test buys run to confirm sequences fire - Live to the real list at end-of-day

Phase Two - Days 4 to 7 - Creator MBA VSL bridge + scorecard ship together

Day 4: - VSL script written (6-9 minutes) and locked with Justin - Scorecard questions scripted (12 questions, score-reveal logic, 3-line readout copy) - Application form Typeform built behind the scorecard 80+ branch

Day 5: - VSL recorded (Justin's setup, half-day shoot) - Scorecard deployed on ScoreApp / Typeform - Routing logic tested (low / mid / high score routes confirmed)

Day 6: - VSL edited - Creator MBA landing page deployed (HTML or Webflow) - Bio routing updated across LinkedIn / X / IG / linktree (scorecard becomes the second primary CTA alongside newsletter)

Day 7: - Newsletter-to-VSL ConvertKit sequence wired (3-7-touch nurture from newsletter signup confirmation routing toward the VSL landing page) - Calendly + application-form 1-1 inquiry route tested end-to-end - Saturday Solopreneur Operator Issue template handed off - Self-serve sales page audit + handoff doc shipped

Risk and mitigation

Risk 01 - Kajabi cross-sell does not support the exact bundle pricing structure Justin wants. Mitigation: Confirm Kajabi bundle capability on Day 1 kickoff working session. If Kajabi native bundle does not match the spec, the bundle runs through a discount-code automation in the ESP plus a duplicate checkout configured for the bundle rate. Either path delivers the same buyer experience.

Risk 02 - ESP is not ConvertKit (beehiiv, Substack, Kit, or other) and the automation playbook needs to flex. Mitigation: Day 1 confirms the ESP. The 5-touch sequences are written platform-agnostic; the automation builder is whichever Justin pays for. If ESP is beehiiv with limited automation, Zapier handles the Kajabi-to-beehiiv handoff.

Risk 03 - Creator MBA Masterclass launch date slips and the VSL bridge sits unused. Mitigation: The VSL bridge does not depend on Creator MBA being live to capture value. The same VSL surface, repointed at the Operating-System courses on the back end (with re-edits), runs as a Saturday Solopreneur VSL pre-sell for LinkedIn OS or Content OS in the interim. The bridge layer earns value whether Creator MBA launches in week 2 or week 12.

Risk 04 - Scorecard low completion rate. Mitigation: Scorecard is split-tested at 8-question and 12-question lengths during Phase Two. Shorter version ships first if completion rate at 12 questions falls below a 40% benchmark on the first 500 starts. Question wording is voice-locked to Justin on Day 4.

Risk 05 - 1-1 inquiry lane inbound volume exceeds Justin's calendar. Mitigation: Application form pre-qualifies hard. Justin sets the cap (5 / 10 / 20 inquiries per quarter is his decision). The high-score routing branch routes overflow to a waitlist confirmation email, not a Calendly link. Justin controls the throughput.

Risk 06 - "Built without ads" brand badge sensitivity. Mitigation: No paid traffic runs in this engagement. Future-phase paid traffic is a separate scope conversation. The pitch on the prospect-side does not promise paid traffic as part of the Day 1 install.

Risk 07 - Kajabi access or technical lock-out. Mitigation: Kajabi admin access requested on Day 1. If access is not granted, AG1 ships specs and Justin's team installs against the specs. AG1 does not require admin access to deliver value, only to execute.
