Script structure

Beat 01 - Hook (0:00 - 0:35)

Open on Justin talking-head. No b-roll. Direct address.

Three lines I want to walk you through.

The first is: most one-person businesses cap somewhere between $50K and $200K a year. They build an audience, they ship a product, they sell some copies, and then they stall.

The second is: the reason they stall is almost never the audience and almost never the product. It is the infrastructure between the two.

The third is: the Creator MBA Masterclass is the install version of the infrastructure I have been writing about in Saturday Solopreneur for the last [N] years. This 7-minute video walks the four pieces.

Beat 02 - The four-stage model (0:35 - 1:50)

Justin talking-head. Cut to b-roll of the stage diagram on screen.

Stage 1 - you have an audience under 10K and no product yet.

Stage 2 - audience is 10K to 100K. One product is live. Maybe a second is forming.

Stage 3 - audience is 100K+. Multiple products are live. The funnel between the audience and the revenue is the bottleneck. This is the stage most of you signed up for the Creator MBA thinking about.

Stage 4 - the operating model compounds for life. The infrastructure is built and earning you back time.

Saturday Solopreneur newsletter readers split roughly across these four stages. The Creator MBA is built for stage 2 and stage 3. If you are stage 1, the Operating-System courses install the audience cadence first.

Beat 03 - The first piece - audience-to-revenue infrastructure (1:50 - 3:10)

Justin talking-head. Cut to b-roll of LinkedIn profile, newsletter signup form, Saturday issue.

Here is the first piece. Most stage-2 and stage-3 solopreneurs have an audience that does not convert to revenue at the rate the audience size says it should.

A 100K LinkedIn following plus a 50K newsletter list plus a $200 self-paced course will earn between $5K and $30K a month in some operators, and between $50K and $200K a month in others. Same audience size, same product price.

The difference is the infrastructure. The bridge between the audience and the product. The post-purchase sequence. The cross-sell at checkout. The application-gated tier above the productised offer.

Creator MBA Module 1 walks the audience-to-revenue infrastructure. By the end of week 2 you have audited your own funnel and named the leak.

Beat 04 - The second piece - the productised stack (3:10 - 4:30)

Justin talking-head. Cut to b-roll of Kajabi storefront, multiple-product layout.

The second piece is the productised stack.

Most stage-2 solopreneurs run one product. The smart ones run two. The compounding ones run three to five, with each product cross-selling the next one at the moment of purchase.

If you have one product, the question is: what does the second one look like and how does the first one cross-sell into it?

If you have two, the question is: where is the order bump? Where is the post-purchase sequence? Where is the bundle?

If you have three, the question is: what is the application-gated tier above the productised offer? Because the audience that wants more than a $200 self-paced course is real, and they are routing nowhere right now.

Creator MBA Module 2 walks the productised stack. By the end of week 4 your second or third product is scoped, and the cross-sell layer between them is mapped.

Beat 05 - The third piece - the operating cadence (4:30 - 5:30)

Justin talking-head. Cut to b-roll of Saturday-morning working setup, calendar block, weekly cadence.

The third piece is the operating cadence.

A one-person business is a discipline. Weekly newsletter. Daily content. Quarterly product motion. The cadence is what compounds when nothing else does.

Most stage-2 solopreneurs try to scale by adding more output. The compounding move is to lock the cadence and let the audience compound underneath it.

Creator MBA Module 3 walks the operating cadence. By the end of week 6 your weekly, monthly, and quarterly rhythm is on a single document you stop second-guessing.

Beat 06 - The fourth piece - the no-headcount operating model (5:30 - 6:30)

Justin talking-head. Direct address.

The fourth piece is the operating model. The thing I get asked about most.

How do you run a [$8M] business with no employees.

The answer is not productivity hacks. The answer is the infrastructure you build, and the things you say no to. The cross-sell layer, the post-purchase sequence, the application-gated tier - these are the things that compound. The team, the headcount, the agency retainer - these are the things that subtract.

Creator MBA Module 4 walks the no-headcount operating model. By the end of week 8 your operating model is on paper, and the next 90 days of execution are scoped.

Beat 07 - The CTA (6:30 - 7:00)

Justin talking-head. Direct address. CTA on screen below.

The Creator MBA Masterclass is the install version of the four pieces.

8 weeks. Self-paced plus a weekly working session. The full thing is on the page below this video.

If you are stage 2 or stage 3, the Creator MBA is the install. If you are stage 1, the Operating-System courses are where you start.

Either way I will see you in the next Saturday issue.

Production specs

Spec Value
Total runtime 6:30 to 7:00 (7 minutes target)
Format Talking-head primary, b-roll cutaways at 30-60 second intervals
Camera Single camera, 4K downsampled to 1080p, eye-level
Audio Lavalier mic, room treatment confirmed pre-shoot
Background Working setup (home office or co-working surface), minimalist, on-brand for "one-person business"
Lighting Soft key from camera-left, fill, no harsh contrast
B-roll LinkedIn profile, newsletter signup, Saturday issue, Kajabi storefront, weekly working cadence shots
Editing pace One cut every 8-12 seconds, brisk but not jumpy
Lower-thirds "Justin Welsh - Creator MBA Masterclass" on hook, no further lower-thirds
Captions Burned-in captions for mute-autoplay environments (LinkedIn embed, Instagram cross-post)
Outro card Creator MBA Masterclass logo + URL + "Apply or enrol below"
Hosting Wistia primary (conversion-rate playback). YouTube unlisted as backup.

B-roll shot list (filmed in the same half-day shoot as the talking-head)

  1. Justin at the working setup, framed wide, Saturday morning light
  2. Close-up of the working setup - laptop, notebook, coffee, no clutter
  3. Justin walking with coffee in a hallway or outdoor space
  4. Hands typing on the LinkedIn post composer
  5. Hands scrolling the Saturday Solopreneur archive
  6. Hands on the Kajabi admin dashboard (screen recorded separately)
  7. Close-up of the calendar block for the weekly working session
  8. Justin reading from the newsletter on a phone
  9. Wide shot of the working setup at dusk (for the "compounding cadence" beat)
  10. Close-up of a printed weekly cadence document

Editing notes

  • Cold-open cut at 0:03 - no logo card, no music swell. Direct address.
  • Music bed: minimalist, unobtrusive, mid-range. Cuts out on key statements.
  • No stock footage. Every cutaway is filmed in Justin's working setup.
  • No on-screen text other than the lower-third at the hook and the outro card. Captions burned-in for accessibility, not as a design element.
  • Outro card holds for 5 seconds with URL on screen, then ends. No "subscribe" plea, no "like and share."
  • File outputs: 16:9 master (Wistia / YouTube), 9:16 vertical 90-sec cut (Instagram / TikTok / LinkedIn vertical), 1:1 90-sec cut (LinkedIn feed embed).
